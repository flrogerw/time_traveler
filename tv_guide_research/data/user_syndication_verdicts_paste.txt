• Circle of Fear: No. Only 22 episodes; rarely seen until cable.
• Ghost Story: No. Only 22 episodes; rarely seen until cable.

• Roots: Special Syndication. Warner Bros. syndicated it to local stations as a multi-night movie event block starting in 1980.
• School House Rock: Educational Syndication. Never syndicated as a standard half-hour show; licensed to schools and later home video/cable.
• Scooby Doo, Where Are You!: Yes. Syndicated heavily throughout the 1970s and 1980s in mixed Hanna-Barbera packages.
• Star Trek: The Animated Series: Yes. Bundled with the original Star Trek or sold in children's afternoon syndication packages in the late 1970s.
• The Prisoner: Cult Syndication. Sold to local independent stations and PBS markets as a weekly cult block throughout the 1970s and 1980s.
• Thriller: Yes. 67 episodes, late-night horror syndication blocks throughout the 1960s and 1970s.
